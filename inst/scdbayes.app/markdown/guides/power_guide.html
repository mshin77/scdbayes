Power estimates how often simulated datasets meet the selected posterior decision rule.

  • Single study: compare case counts and baseline/intervention observation counts.
  • Meta-analysis: compare study counts and cases per study. Category comparisons also depend on studies in each category.
  • Interpretation: estimates depend on the assumed effect, variability, model, and decision rule. More simulation replicates reduce Monte Carlo uncertainty.

Kruschke, J. K., & Liddell, T. M. (2018). The Bayesian new statistics: Hypothesis testing, estimation, meta-analysis, and power analysis from a Bayesian perspective. Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 25(1), 178–206. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-016-1221-4
